## Pronunciation list

The run's 1002 refusals hit most of this group, so the 5 refused requests
were rerun 6 s apart afterwards (all returned audio). One comparison page
for listening: `.data/p5b-check/names-compare.html`.

- **Billing:** no rule added anything. 我是日和，今天也请多指教。 bills 24
  with or without `日和/(ri4)(he2)`; 大家好，我是Hiyori。 bills 18 with no
  rule, with the pinyin rule and with ひより.
- **Subtitles:** offsets still index the text as sent (max `text_end` 13); a
  replaced word repeats its key once per synthesized unit (Hiyori ×5 with
  the pinyin rule, ×3 with ひより).
- **What the timings suggest (the ear decides):** with no rule, Hiyori is
  split as H / i / y / or / i, i.e. read out letter by letter; the pinyin
  rule gives two short syllables and ひより three.
- **Decision (the user, by ear):** keep `日和/(ri4)(he2)` and
  `Hiyori/(ri4)(he2)`, the shipped rules; ひより is not used.
- **Full-width brackets are not performed:** 这是（he2）平 is spoken as the
  literal "he" and "2", so mapping reply brackets to full width is enough and
  the fallback (deleting such groups) is not needed.

after about 14 accepted requests in 25 s; after that, 2 of the next 8 got

stream plus the web chat share this key. Check the account's RPM in the
MiniMax console before the first stream; today a 1002 reaches the stage as
`TTS_UPSTREAM_INVALID` (logged locally as `status-code-1002`) and the turn
ends partial or failed without a retry.
